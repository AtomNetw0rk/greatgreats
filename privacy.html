Privacy Policy

GreatGreats Publisher · last updated 17 November 2026

GreatGreats Publisher is a private tool used only by the creators of The Great Greats. It uploads our own animated episodes, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to our own accounts on YouTube and other social platforms. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data the tool uses

  • The access tokens we grant it for our own accounts, so it can upload videos, set thumbnails, add videos to our playlists and post a comment on our own videos.
  • Basic details of our own channel (its ID and title), to confirm it posts to the right account.

It does not collect, store or share any data about viewers or any other person.

Where the data is kept

Tokens are stored encrypted on the creator's own computer only. They are never sent to any server other than the platform they belong to, and they are never sold or shared.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in each platform's app settings. The local tokens are then deleted.
